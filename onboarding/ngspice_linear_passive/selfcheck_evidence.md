## c. RC .ac corner — and what actually sets its error

R=1k, C=159.155n, analytic corner **999.9996424 Hz**.

Grid density, as requested (sweep from 10 Hz):

| points/decade | corner (Hz) | rel. vs analytic |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | 1000.095291 | +9.565e-05 |
| 100 | 1000.099080 | +9.944e-05 |

Delta between the two grids: **0.003789 Hz (3.789e-06 relative)**.

**Finding — the grid delta is NOT this QoI's error bar.** Neither grid agrees with
the analytic corner to better than ~1e-4, and refining 10x barely moved it. The
cause is `fstart`: |H_dc| is proxied by |H| at the lowest solved frequency, biasing
a one-pole corner high by a relative (fstart/fc)². Measured against that prediction:

| fstart | corner (Hz) | rel. error | predicted (fstart/fc)² |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10.0 Hz | 1000.099080 | +9.944e-05 | 1.000e-04 |
| 1.0 Hz | 1000.000638 | +9.960e-07 | 1.000e-06 |
| 0.1 Hz | 999.999650 | +7.511e-09 | 1.000e-08 |

Prediction matches measurement to three significant figures, and the bias at
fstart=10 Hz is **~26x larger** than the grid delta it sits inside. Start an AC sweep
at least three decades below the expected corner.
