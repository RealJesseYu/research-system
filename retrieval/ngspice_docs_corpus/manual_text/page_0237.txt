Due to the fact that time steps are not constant during a transient simulation, but op-
timized by the simulator, the delayed values are sometimes slightly deviating from the
original, depending on the number of steps. So in a sinusoidal wave we will see a distortion
< 0.3% for 1000 steps per sin cycle.
