15.3.5 .OP: Operating Point Analysis
General form:
. op
Compute the DC operating point of the circuit with inductors shorted and capacitors
opened.
A DC solution can be difficult to find for some circuits, including those with floating nodes
or active devices that are non-conducting. After an attempt at an initial DC solution
(may be suppressed by .option noopiter ), ngspice uses the following convergence aids,
in order, to try to obtain a DC solution:
1. gmin stepping (gminsteps option). Inserts small conductances across active devices.
• gminsteps = 0: No gmin
• gminsteps = 1: Two gmin stepping processes in series (default)
• gminsteps = 2: Original SPICE 3 gmin
2. source stepping (srcsteps option)
