12.4.1 Buffer
NAME_TABLE:
C_Function_Name: cm_d_buffer
Spice_Model_Name: d_buffer
Description: "digital one-bit-wide buffer"
PORT_TABLE:
Port Name: in out
Description: "input" "output"
Direction: in out
Default_Type: d d
Allowed_Types: [d] [d]
Vector: no no
Vector_Bounds: - -
Null_Allowed: no no
Common parameters: inertial_delay, family, rise_delay, fall_delay, input_load.
Description: The buffer is a single-input, single-output digital buffer that produces as
output a time-delayed copy of its input.
Example SPICE Usage:
a6 1 8 buff1
.model buff1 d_buffer(rise_delay = 0.5e-9 fall_delay = 0.3e-9
+ input_load = 0.5e-12)
