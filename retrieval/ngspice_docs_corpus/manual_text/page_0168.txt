11.2.8 BSIM1 model (level 4)
BSIM1 model (the first is a long series) is an empirical model. Developers placed less
emphasis on device physics and based the model on parametrical polynomial equations
to model the various physical effects. This approach pays in terms of circuit simulation
behavior but the accuracy degrades in the submicron region. A known problem of this
model is the negative output conductance and the convergence problems, both related to
poor behavior of the polynomial equations.
