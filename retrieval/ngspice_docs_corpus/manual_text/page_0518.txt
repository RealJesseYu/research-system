21.5 Four-Bit Binary Adder (Bipolar)
The following deck simulates a four-bit binary adder, using several subcircuits to describe
various pieces of the overall circuit.
Example:
ADDER - 4 BIT ALL - NAND - GATE BINARY ADDER
*** SUBCIRCUIT DEF INI TIO NS
. SUBCKT NAND 1 2 3 4
* NODES : INPUT (2) , OUTPUT , VCC
Q1 9 5 1 QMOD
D1CLAMP 0 1 DMOD
Q2 9 5 2 QMOD
D2CLAMP 0 2 DMOD
RB 4 5 4 K
R1 4 6 1.6 K
Q3 6 9 8 QMOD
R2 8 0 1 K
RC 4 7 130
Q4 7 6 10 QMOD
DVBEDROP 10 3 DMOD
Q5 3 8 0 QMOD
. ENDS NAND
