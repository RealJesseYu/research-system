9.2.3 JFET level 2 Parker Skellern model
The level 2 model is an improvement to level 1. Details are available in a pdf originating
from Macquarie University. Some important items are
• The description maintains strict continuity in its high-order derivatives, which is
essential for prediction of distortion and intermodulation.
• Frequency dependence of output conductance and transconductance is described as
a function of bias.
• Both drain-gate and source-gate potentials modulate the pinch-off potential, which
is consistent with S-parameter and pulsed-bias measurements.
• Self-heating varies with frequency.
• Extreme operating regions - subthreshold, forward gate bias, controlled resistance,
and breakdown regions - are included.
• Parameters provide independent fitting to all operating regions. It is not necessary
to compromise one region in favor of another.
• Strict drain-source symmetry is maintained. The transition during drain-source
potential reversal is smooth and continuous.
The model equations are described in this pdf document and in [ 19].
