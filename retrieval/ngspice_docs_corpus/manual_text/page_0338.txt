15.6.5.2 (Differential) voltage measurement
Differential voltage measurements are achieved by placing a voltage controlled voltage
source (VCVS, E device) with its two inputs connected to the nodes specified by the user
and gain 1. The output is then saved in a vector with a leading vd_ in its name.
