15.7 Measuring current through device terminals
15.7.1 Using the .probe command
Device currents (discrete devices or subcircuits) may be measured by the .probe command
(15.6.5). Voltage sources for measurements are placed in series to the devices nodes
specified by the user. For details please see ( 15.6.5).
15.7.2 Adding a voltage source in series
The ngspice matrix solver determines node voltages and currents through independent
voltage sources. So to measure the currents through a resistor, you may add a voltage
source in series with dc voltage 0.

* measure current through R1
V1 1 0 1
R1 1 0 5
R2 1 0 10

V1 1 0 1
R1 1 11 5
