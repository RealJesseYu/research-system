22.4 ngspice scripting language
The ngspice scripting language is described in detail in Chapt. 17.8. All commands listed
in Chapt. 17.5 are available, as well as the built-in functions described in Chapt. 17.2, the
control structures listed in Chapt. 17.6, and the predefined variables from Chapt. 17.7.
Variables and functions are typically evaluated after a simulation run. You may created
loops with several simulation runs and change device and model parameters with the
alter (17.5.3) or altermod (17.5.4) commands, as shown in the next section 22.5. You
may even interrupt a simulation run by proper usage of the stop (17.5.88) and resume
(17.5.64) commands. After stop you may change device or model parameters and then
go on with resume, continuing the simulation with the new parameter values.
