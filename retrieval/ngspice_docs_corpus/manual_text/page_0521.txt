21.7 Transmission-Line Inverter
The following deck simulates a transmission-line inverter. Two transmission-line elements
are required since two propagation modes are excited. In the case of a coaxial line, the
first line (T1) models the inner conductor with respect to the shield, and the second line
(T2) models the shield with respect to the outside world.
